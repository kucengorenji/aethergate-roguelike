## codex-kit-ruins.png

- Date: 2026-10-27
- Tool: built-in image_gen
- Critique: Strong modular silhouettes and matching stone palette; masonry texture is denser than a final low-poly mesh should carry.

Exact prompt:

```text
Create a WIDE LANDSCAPE environment kit concept sheet for Aethergate, an original dark fantasy third-person spell shooter. Art direction: painterly low-poly game assets matching a fallen golden age, pale ash-beige and grey-brown stone, muted warm gold highlights, thin dark edges, chunky faceted planes with soft painted gradients, strong silhouettes, simplified enough to build as modular meshes. Plain flat MID-GREY studio background, no scene or horizon. Consistent orthographic-like three-quarter camera and even soft studio light on every piece. Lay out distinct objects in a tidy evenly spaced grid, separated by generous empty grey space, all wholly visible, no overlaps: (1) straight ruined wall module with flat snap ends, (2) broken wall module, (3) L-shaped corner wall, (4) intact pillar, (5) broken pillar, (6) fallen pillar drum, (7) pointed stone arch, (8) separate doorway exit arch with faint GOLDEN glow filling its opening, (9) two distinct square floor tile variants, (10) low rubble pile, (11) two different small gravestones, (12) one twisted tree-root cluster, (13) iron brazier with warm orange fire, (14) three SMALL floating reward tokens: golden blessing orb, pale-blue shard cluster, red healing flask. Ensure all requested pieces are present, countable, and visibly separate. Stone components have compatible thickness and masonry proportions so they could tile and snap together. Big shapes, low detail; no finely carved filigree, tiny cracks, scene painting, humans, words, letters, logos, watermarks, or any borrowed game designs.
```

- Date: 2026-10-27
- Tool: built-in image_gen
- Critique: All four props and the separate tree backdrop read clearly; the skyline card is more detailed than the buildable props.

Exact prompt:

```text
Create a WIDE LANDSCAPE Kindled Hall environment asset sheet for Aethergate, original dark fantasy. Match the approved painterly low-poly direction: pale ash-beige and grey-brown stone, thin dark edges, broad flat faceted planes with soft painted gradients, muted warm gold, pale blue magic, intimate ruin scale, simplified shapes that can be built as low-poly game meshes. Below it, arrange FOUR separate three-quarter-view objects in one roomy row, each wholly visible with large gaps: (1) a small grace site: delicate sapling of golden light with a few branching limbs, growing from a low circular broken stone ring; (2) compact upgrade altar of faceted ash-beige stone with EXACTLY ONE pale-blue crystal floating above its center; (3) simple dark iron and aged wood staff rack holding EXACTLY THREE distinct staffs upright, one with a pale-blue crystal and two with modest plain heads; (4) short broken hall wall with a SINGLE pointed window arch, weathered pale stone, simple clean masonry snap ends. Keep props small and intimate relative to the skyline card. The backdrop card must read as flat environment art, not a physical model. Even soft studio lighting on props, warm magical bloom only on sapling and crystal. No characters, extra staffs, extra crystals, other props, photorealism, tiny filigree, text, labels, logos, watermark, or borrowed game designs.
```
